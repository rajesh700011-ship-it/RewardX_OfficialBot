About QuickTools

Simple tools, honest software

QuickTools provides simple, reliable productivity tools for everyday use — a QR code generator, password generator, calculator, unit converter, text counter, color picker, and password strength checker, all in one place.

The app is built to be fast, free, and easy to use. There is no account to create and no unnecessary steps between you and the tool you need.

Why QuickTools

  • Everything runs directly in your browser — most tools work without any internet connection once loaded.
  • No sign-up, no clutter, no confusing menus.
  • Designed for quick, everyday tasks on mobile.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Our Approach

QuickTools is built around one simple rule: we ask for as little of your data as possible. Most tools run entirely on your device — text you type, passwords you generate, and QR codes you create are processed locally and are never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

What We Don't Store

  • We do not store generated passwords.
  • We do not store QR code content.
  • We do not store calculator inputs or results.
  • We do not require an account, email, or phone number to use the app.

Local Preferences

Some non-sensitive preferences — such as your favorite tools, dark mode setting, and a short list of recently opened tools — may be saved locally on your own device so the app remembers your choices. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Third-Party Services & Advertising

If advertising is enabled within QuickTools, it will always be optional and clearly presented — never forced or misleading. Any advertising or analytics provider we use may collect limited, non-identifying technical information (such as general device type) in line with their own privacy policies. We do not sell personal data.

Data Security

Because most processing happens locally on your device, there is minimal data in transit or in storage for us to protect. Where data is processed (such as optional advertising), we rely on reputable, industry-standard providers.

Your Rights

You can clear your locally stored preferences, favorites, and history at any time from the Settings page. This action is immediate and permanent.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at privacy@quicktools.app.
